Alfa Yohannis is an assistant professor (Asisten Ahli) in the Department of Informatics at Pradita University, Indonesia. Yohannis received a PhD from the University of York in 2020, a Master of Engineering in Informatics from Institut Teknologi Bandung in 2009, and a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering from Universitas Diponegoro in 2006. The research of Yohannis covers model-driven engineering, in particular change-based model persistence, model comparison and the exploration of complex models, together with decentralized data vaults and blockchain applications. Yohannis also studies information technology strategy for higher education.
