Highlights

- An EMF resource stores models on IPFS and takes each saved content identifier as URI
- References by content identifier pin the exact version of the target resource
- The cascade save keeps references current on acyclic structures but not on cycles
- A save through a local IPFS node added 40 to 43 ms, mostly waiting for the disk
- On 11,486 real BPMN models, IPFS added no failure, while XMI and random ids did
